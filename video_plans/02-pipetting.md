## Why film this

The page teaches a six-step motion from **one still image** and then requires a hands-on
demonstration to an instructor. Everything between the diagram and the demo is currently
left to the student to infer. First stop vs. second stop is the single hardest thing to
convey in text and the single easiest thing to convey on camera — the plunger has two
detents and a still image cannot show the resistance change between them.

## Shots

Shoot tight. This is a hands-and-tips video; the operator's face is irrelevant. Use a
macro or close framing throughout, over-the-shoulder or directly down onto the bench.

1. **The four pipettes in a row** (~30 s) — P10, P20, P200, P1000 with ranges on screen:
   0.5–10, 0.5/2–20, 20–200, 100–1000 µL. Say: use the smallest pipette whose range covers
   your volume.

2. **Reading the display** (~30 s) — the page's exact example. Show a P20 reading `200`
   and say "twenty point zero microliters." Show a P1000 reading `200` and say "two hundred
   microliters." Same digits, different meaning. Hold both on screen together if possible.

3. **LTS vs universal tips** (~30 s) — show an LTS tip seating with light pressure. Show a
   universal tip failing to seal. Say it will give inaccurate volumes.

4. **The six-step sequence** (~90 s) — the core shot. Narrate each step as the hands do it:
   1. Set the volume with the dial, within range
   2. Attach a sterile tip — press into the box, gentle twist
   3. Press the plunger **to the first stop** — *hold here, name the stop*
   4. Immerse **2–3 mm** below the surface; release the plunger **slowly**
   5. Touch the tip to the destination wall; press **to the second stop**
   6. Eject with the ejector button

   **The money shot:** a very close side view of the thumb finding first stop, pausing, then
   pushing through to second stop. Consider a slow-motion or repeated pass on this alone.

5. **Confirming by eye** (~20 s) — the page says to visually check how far liquid rides up
   the tip. Show a correct 0.5 µL, then a failed one, side by side.

6. **When things go wrong** (~45 s) — deliberately produce all four failure signs and hold
   each on camera: air bubbles in the tip; liquid stuck in the tip; droplets on the outside
   of the tip; inconsistent replicates. Then discard and redo. Showing the wrong thing on
   purpose is the highest-value 45 seconds in this video.

7. **P1000 held vertically** (~30 s) — show liquid running toward the barrel when tilted.
   Say it damages the mechanism. Mention the apparent air gap at large volumes is gravity,
   not an error.

8. **Contamination discipline** (~45 s) — one tip, one sample. Show the *correct* setup
   order explicitly: label tubes, open caps, arrange racks, uncap reagents, **then** pick up
   a tip. Then show the wrong way — fumbling a cap with a loaded tip — and name why it is
   a contamination risk.

9. **The graded demo, start to finish** (~60 s) — reproduce exactly what students must
   perform, in order, because this is what they will be marked on:
   1. 1 mL water, conical → 1.5 mL tube, P1000
   2. 50 µL pink solution → 0.2 mL PCR tube, P200
   3. 0.5 µL blue glycerol → same PCR tube, P20
   4. Close the PCR tube **once** — say that repeated openings damage the seal and cause
      leaks during thermocycling
   5. Mix: invert and slam upside down on the bench
   6. Quick spin

   Close on: "This is the demonstration you will perform for your instructor."

## Props

P10/P20/P200/P1000, LTS tips, one universal tip for the failure shot, conical of water,
pink solution, blue glycerol solution, 1.5 mL tubes, 0.2 mL PCR tubes, racks, minifuge,
waste bin.

The dyed solutions matter — they make small volumes visible on camera in a way clear water
cannot. The blue glycerol also shows viscous-liquid technique, which is why the page picked
it for the 0.5 µL step.

## Watch out

- Every volume spoken must match the page exactly. Students are graded against the page.
- Do not lay a loaded P1000 on the bench, even between takes — someone will screenshot it.

## Quiz impact

None required. The existing bank covers pipette selection, tip changes, workflow order,
LTS tips, and P1000 handling — all of which the video demonstrates.
