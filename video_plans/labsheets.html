Preparation of LB Agar (250 mL bottles)

agar_fresh

  • 3 × 250 mL bottles, plus spare empties
  • 1 level tablespoon powder per bottle
  • DI to the neck line · cap · shake
  • Cooker: Beef (small) / Sterilize (large)
  • 20 min high pressure · natural release
  • Cool to ~55 °C

Preparation of 2YT Broth (250 mL bottles)

broth_2yt

  • 4 × 250 mL bottles
  • Premixed 2YT powder — check the scoop on the tub
  • or components: 16 / 10 / 5 g/L tryptone / yeast ext / NaCl
  • DI to the neck line · shake until clear
  • 20 min high pressure · natural release
  • Cooker holds 5 bottles → 3 agar + 4 broth = 2 runs

Preparation of Antibiotic 1000× Stock

abx_erm

Erythromycin

  • 100 mg/mL
  • µL = mg × 10
  • Solvent: ethanol — will not go into water at all
  • Scoop ~50 mg · vortex until clear, it is slow
  • Crashes out of hot agar, then redissolves

Re-melting Solidified LB Agar

agar_remelt

  • 1 × 250 mL bottle
  • Loosen the lid a quarter turn
  • 30–60 s bursts · rest between
  • Stop at boiling
  • Cool to ~55 °C
