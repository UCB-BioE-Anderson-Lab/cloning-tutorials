## Why film this

Same argument as Zymo: every step is a spin, and the failure mode is losing the DNA.
Miniprep adds two things Zymo doesn't have and that both need showing:

- **Mixing gently and not vortexing** during alkaline lysis. Vortexing shears genomic DNA
  into the prep and ruins it. "Invert to mix" is a specific motion.
- **Glove contamination.** The page warns that crusty debris from bottle rims transfers to
  gloves and then to samples. That is a visual problem with a visual fix.

## Shots

1. **Why miniprep** (~30 s) — storage at −20 °C, DNA as a building block, sequencing
   template, retransformation, quantification. One line each, over `miniprep_steps.png`.

2. **The chemistry in one breath** (~30 s) — same guanidinium-silica idea as the Zymo, but
   starting from cells instead of a PCR. Lyse → clarify → bind → wash → elute.

3. **Alkaline lysis** (~2 min) — the segment that matters:
   - **Pellet** 1–5 mL of saturated culture. Show pouring off the supernatant completely —
     leftover media carries into the prep
   - **250 µL P1** — resuspend **fully**. Show a properly resuspended pellet, then show an
     incompletely resuspended one with visible clumps and say it costs yield. Note RNase A
     must already be in the P1 and that it lives in the fridge afterwards
   - **250 µL P2** — **invert gently, do NOT vortex.** Hold this shot. Show the lysate going
     clear and viscous — that visual is how you know lysis worked
   - **350 µL N3** — invert to mix thoroughly. Show the white precipitate forming
   - **Spin 5 min** at max speed. Show the compact debris pellet and the clear supernatant

4. **Column binding and washing** (~90 s):
   - Supernatant to the **blue QIAprep column**, spin 15 s
   - **500 µL PB**, spin
   - **750 µL PE**, spin
   - Discard flowthrough, **spin again 90 s to dry**
   - Say at each step where the DNA is, same discipline as the Zymo video

5. **Elution** (~30 s) — column into a **new** 1.5 mL tube, **50 µL EB to the centre of the
   membrane**, spin 45 s. Same centre-of-membrane close-up as the Zymo video.

6. **Glove hygiene** (~30 s) — the page's own warning, filmed. Show debris on a bottle rim,
   show it on a glove, show rinsing gloves at the sink. Say this procedure is messy and that
   contaminated gloves contaminate samples.

7. **Label and inventory** (~30 s) — **top and side** of each tube with the clone ID
   (`pP6-79A`). Into the `minis1` box. Record the box position (e.g. `E3`) on the lab sheet.
   Say minipreps are retained and must be inventoried — this is not optional bookkeeping.

## Watch out

- **P2 precipitates when cold.** If the bottle is cloudy on the day, that is a teachable
  moment worth filming: warm it gently until clear before use.
- **PE needs ethanol added before first use.** Show the checkmark on the cap convention.
- Do not vortex anything on camera at any point in this video.
