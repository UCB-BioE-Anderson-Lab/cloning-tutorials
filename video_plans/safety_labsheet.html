Take 1 — lab_tour · one continuous walking shot, quiet room

Name every location out loud as you reach it. A tour is entirely information carried by the picture; anything unsaid is absent from the captions and invisible to a screen reader.

  1. Entry. Only enter when a supervisor is present.
  2. Safety Corner — by the north door, near the biosafety cabinets on the mammalian cell side. Slow down here. Safety shower · eye wash · fire extinguisher (all three by the biohood-side door) · spill kit · first aid kit · Chemical Hygiene Plan with emergency information · PPE stock · the printed Lab Rules, whose full list is the posted PDF. Say you must know how to operate the extinguisher safely. Say “fifteen minutes” at the eye wash and hold the shot.
  3. Benches — where students work; the default organisation they must restore.
  4. Electrophoresis bench — gel rig drawer, 1× TAE, imager.
  5. Thermocyclers — ProFlex.
  6. Freezers — enzyme freezer, −20, −80. Point out the pink Training1 box.
  7. Chemical hood — flame sterilization happens here, supervisors only.
  8. Microwave.
  9. Waste — large white bin, benchtop plastic beakers.
  10. Exit route — walk to the door and say evacuation goes to the Mining Circle. Keep walking outside and show it if you can.

Take 2 — biosafety · static, at a bench

Say these six close to the wording below. Each maps onto a quiz question.

  • Flush your eyes for fifteen minutes
  • Evacuate to the Mining Circle
  • No food or drink anywhere in B144 — not even a capped water bottle
  • Never spray ethanol near an open flame
  • Prescription glasses count as eye protection
  • Supervisors Only means do not touch it

General conduct

  • No food or drink in the lab
  • Enter only when a supervisor — facilitator or instructor — is present
  • Only projects authorised by your instructor or PI
  • No materials in or out unless explicitly authorised
  • BSL1 only — no infectious agents, no sharps, no hazardous chemicals

PPE — required at all times

  • Closed-toe shoes and long pants · lab coat · gloves · eye protection
  • Prescription glasses are acceptable eye protection
  • PPE lives in the Safety Corner
  • Show one person correctly dressed, head to toe, in a single shot

Hazards

  • Microwave — superheated agar or liquids can burn you
  • Bunsen burner — fire hazard, especially with ethanol-based solutions
  • Never spray ethanol near an open flame

Signage — three tiers

  • Supervisors Only — students must not touch or use it
  • Training Required — allowed only after the training; follow the QR on the sign
  • Training Available — scan the QR if you don't know how to use it
  • If in doubt, ask your supervisor before using anything
  • Scan one QR on camera so the mechanism is seen working

Emergencies

  • Notify your supervisor immediately of any spill or injury
  • 911 for life-threatening emergencies
  • Evacuation goes to the Mining Circle

Cleanup, records, compliance

  • Clean the bench and restore its default organisation after every session
  • Research materials including gloves → large white bin or benchtop beakers
  • Keep accurate records of all experiments as directed
  • Follow University policies, including COVID-19 mitigation and emergency response
  • Breaking these rules can cost lab access

On the day

  • Do not pull the safety shower. Point at it, say what it is for, move on.
  • Film when the room is quiet. Anyone incidentally on camera should know they are being filmed.
  • Say each slug aloud as you hold up the card — it segments the transcript for you later.
