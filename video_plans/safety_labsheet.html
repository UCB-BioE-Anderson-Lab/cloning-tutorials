Take 1 — lab_tour · one continuous walking shot, quiet room

Name every location out loud as you reach it. A tour is entirely information carried by the picture; anything unsaid is absent from the captions and invisible to a screen reader.

  1. Entry. Only enter when a supervisor is present.
  2. Safety Corner — east wall, near the northeast corner, north side of the room, by the biosafety cabinets. Slow down here. Safety shower · eye wash · fire extinguisher · spill kit · first aid kit · Chemical Hygiene Plan binder and posted emergency numbers · PPE stock · the printed Lab Rules. Say “fifteen minutes” at the eye wash and hold the shot.
  3. Benches — where students work; the default organisation they must restore.
  4. Electrophoresis bench — gel rig drawer, 1× TAE, imager.
  5. Thermocyclers — ProFlex.
  6. Freezers — enzyme freezer, −20, −80. Point out the pink Training1 box.
  7. Chemical hood — flame sterilization happens here, supervisors only.
  8. Microwave.
  9. Waste — large white bin, benchtop plastic beakers.
  10. Exit route — walk to the door and say evacuation goes to the Mining Circle. Keep walking outside and show it if you can.

Take 2 — biosafety · static, at a bench

Say these six close to the wording below. Each maps onto a quiz question.

  • Flush your eyes for fifteen minutes
  • Evacuate to the Mining Circle
  • No food or drink anywhere in B144 — not even a capped water bottle
  • Never spray ethanol near an open flame
  • Prescription glasses count as eye protection
  • Supervisors Only means do not touch it

Conduct

  • Supervisor present; authorised projects only
  • Nothing in or out
  • BSL1 only — no infectious agents, sharps, or hazardous chemicals

PPE

  • Closed-toe shoes, long pants, lab coat, gloves, eye protection
  • Show one person correctly dressed, head to toe, in a single shot

Signage — three tiers

  • Supervisors Only — do not touch
  • Training Required — only after training; scan the QR
  • Training Available — scan if unsure
  • Scan one QR on camera so the mechanism is seen working

Hazards · emergencies · cleanup

  • Superheated agar and liquids in the microwave; burner fire risk
  • Report every spill and injury immediately; 911 if life-threatening
  • Restore the bench; gloves and waste to the white bin
  • Violations cost lab access

On the day

  • Do not pull the safety shower. Point at it, say what it is for, move on.
  • Film when the room is quiet. Anyone incidentally on camera should know they are being filmed.
  • Say each slug aloud as you hold up the card — it segments the transcript for you later.
