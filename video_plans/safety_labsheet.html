Make each point where the object is. Every rule is attached to the thing it is about, so it lands while the viewer is looking at it. Name every station out loud as you reach it — a tour is entirely information carried by the picture, and anything unsaid is absent from the captions and invisible to a screen reader. Red = say close to this wording; each maps onto a quiz question.

  1. The door — the magnet
    What the magnet means, and when you may and may not come in Enter only when a supervisor — facilitator or instructor — is present “No food or drink anywhere in B144 — not even a capped water bottle” Only projects authorised by your instructor or PI Nothing in or out unless explicitly authorised BSL1 only. No infectious agents · no sharps · no hazardous chemicals “Evacuate to the Mining Circle” — show the route while you are at the door
  2. The three bays
    What lives on the surfaces, and what lives in the drawers below The map on the wall, and the same map online Restore your bench to its default organisation after every session Keep accurate records of all experiments as directed
  3. Safety corner
    Goggles, gloves, lab coats — all PPE lives here Required at all times: closed-toe shoes and long pants · lab coat · gloves · eye protection “Prescription glasses count as eye protection” EHS onboarding — what it is, and that it is required Safety shower · eye wash · fire extinguisher, all by the biohood-side door “Flush your eyes for fifteen minutes” — hold the shot on the eye wash You must know how to operate the extinguisher safely Spill kit · first aid kit · Chemical Hygiene Plan with emergency information Tell your supervisor immediately about any spill or injury; 911 if life-threatening The printed Lab Rules; the full list is the posted PDF Do not pull the shower — point, say what it is for, move on
  4. Media bench & microwave
    Microwave — superheated agar or liquids can burn you Training Required — allowed only after the training; follow the QR on the sign Scan one QR on camera so the mechanism is seen working Training Available — scan it if you don't know how to use something If in doubt, ask your supervisor before using anything
