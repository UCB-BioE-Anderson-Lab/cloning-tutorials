# 1. Lab Safety + Lab Tour

- **Page:** `docs/wetlab/safety.md`
- **Proposed ID:** `pP6-2026-1-Safety`
- **Prior version:** none usable — the 2022 series started at PCR, and any older
  safety/tour footage is **room-linked to Donner**, so it shows the wrong building
- **Runtime:** 6–8 min (the longest in the series; it is doing two jobs)
- **Advance prep:** none. Shootable cold.
- **Hosting:** YouTube unlisted — this content is reached by QR from wall signage

## Why film this

**Nothing can be reused here.** The lab is now B144 Stanley; prior safety and tour footage
was shot in Donner and would send students to rooms and corners that no longer exist. This
is the one video in the series with no ancestor at all, and it is also the highest-
consequence content on the site.

Text can name the eye wash. Only film shows *where it is* and *how far you are from it*.
This is the highest-consequence content on the site, and it is the one place where the
written page is structurally unable to do the job.

Doubling as the lab tour is the right call: a new student's real question is not "what are
the rules" but "where is everything," and no page currently answers that.

## Structure

Two halves. Walk the room first, then cover the rules that attach to what was just seen.
The tour motivates the rules rather than the rules preceding an abstract room.

### Part A — The tour (~3 min, one continuous walking shot if possible)

Walk the room in physical order. Say each location out loud as you reach it.

1. **Entry / door.** State the entry rule as you walk in: only enter when a supervisor is
   present. Establishes the room and the first rule in one beat.
2. **Safety Corner** — the anchor of the whole video. Slow down here.
   - Safety shower — pull handle, what it is for
   - Eye wash — **say "fifteen minutes" out loud and hold the shot on it**
   - Fire extinguisher
   - Spill kit, first aid kit
   - Chemical Hygiene Plan binder and posted emergency numbers
   - PPE stock: gloves, lab coats, eye protection
   - The printed Lab Rules copy posted here
3. **Benches** — where students work, default bench organization (needed later for the
   "restore it" rule).
4. **Electrophoresis bench** — gel rig drawer, 1× TAE bottle, imager.
5. **Thermocyclers** — ProFlex block.
6. **Freezers** — enzyme freezer, −20, −80. Note the pink `Training1` box; PCR references it.
7. **Chemical hood** — note it is where flame sterilization happens, supervisors only.
8. **Microwave.**
9. **Waste** — large white bin, benchtop plastic beakers.
10. **Exit route** — walk to the door and say where evacuation goes: **Mining Circle**.
    If practical, keep walking outside and show it. That single shot is worth more than
    the sentence on the page.

### Part B — The rules (~3–4 min, static, at a bench)

Track the page section order so the video and page reinforce each other:

- **General conduct** — no food or drink; supervisor present; authorized projects only;
  nothing in or out; **BSL1 only — no infectious agents, sharps, or hazardous chemicals**
- **PPE** — closed-toe shoes, long pants, lab coat, gloves, eye protection (prescription
  glasses acceptable). Show a correctly dressed person head to toe in one shot.
- **Signage** — hold up or walk to each of the three signs and **scan one QR on camera**
  so students see the mechanism work:
  - Supervisors Only → do not touch
  - Training Required → allowed only after training; scan the QR
  - Training Available → scan if you don't know the instrument
- **Common hazards** — microwave superheated agar and liquids; Bunsen burner fire risk;
  **never spray ethanol near an open flame**
- **Emergencies** — report every spill or injury immediately; 911 for life-threatening;
  evacuate to Mining Circle
- **Cleanup** — restore bench to default organization; gloves and research waste to the
  white bin or benchtop beakers
- **Compliance** — rules exist to keep lab access; violations can cost it

## Must-say lines

These map directly onto quiz questions. Say them in words close to the page.

- "Flush your eyes for **fifteen minutes**." (quiz marks "15 seconds" false)
- "Evacuate to the **Mining Circle**."
- "**No food or drink anywhere in B144** — not even a capped water bottle."
- "**Never spray ethanol near an open flame.**"
- "Prescription glasses count as eye protection."
- "'Supervisors Only' means do not touch it."

## Watch out

- **Location wording.** The page says the Safety Corner is "by the north door, near the
  biosafety cabinets on the mammalian cell side." The flame page says "northeast corner by
  the door." The quiz marks "south-most door" as false. Use the page's wording so nothing
  contradicts the quiz — and since the camera resolves it visually anyway, consider
  tightening the page to one phrasing afterward.
- **Do not demonstrate the safety shower by pulling it.** Point, describe, move on.
- Film when the room is quiet. Anyone incidentally on camera should know they are being
  filmed.

## Quiz impact

No new questions needed — the video covers exactly the existing eight topics. If anything,
the tour makes the location questions fairer, since students will have actually seen the
corner rather than read a sentence about it.
