Sealing Petri Dishes with Parafilm

parafilm

DAY 2 · the plates you are keeping

  • These plates are being held for picking later — the seal is what makes that possible
  • Cut a 1-inch strip along the long edge of the roll
  • Hold against the lid rim · stretch until it grips
  • Spin the plate, keeping tension, to wrap the seam
  • Press the end down onto itself to seal
  • Inverted in the fridge · stable 1–2 weeks
  • Get back to them within the week — satellites keep growing and the green dims
  • Never parafilm anything going into the incubator — cells need air

Tecan the BestP block

bestp_tecan

DAY 3 · Tecan M Nano · 48 read wells

  • Check the cultures grew and are fluorescent before anything else
  • 100 µL per culture into a black-walled 96-well plate
  • P200 multichannel · columns of 8 · 2 draws per sample
  • Fluorescence: Ex 483 / Em 525, bandwidths 20, gain 40, 20 reads
  • high sensitivity · 40 µs · Z 11019 µm · 30 °C · COS96ft
  • Also read OD600 — fluorescence alone is uninterpretable
  • Save to the USB stick; that computer has no internet
  • Remove your plate before shutdown · bleach and rinse it

What BestP is measuring

bestp_intro

WHENEVER · no bench work

  • RPU = fluorescence per OD unit, normalised to pJ01 = 1
  • pJ12 very weak · pJ01 the reference · pJ19 very strong
  • Same backbone, same reporter — so only the promoter differs
  • Raw fluorescence is not comparable across runs; RPU is
  • The goal: pP6 clones that match or beat pJ19
