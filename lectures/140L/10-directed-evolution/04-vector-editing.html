Vector editing

  1. Design the prototype plasmid with clear part boundaries
  2. For each part, define a family of edits (the vector)
  3. Make each single part edit variant of the prototype in parallel
  4. Test each and find the best one (currbest)
  5. Repeat 3 with currbest
  • For example, replace the ORF encoding the GGPP synthase with 8 different orthologs from other organisms
  • Or, replace a promoter with 6 of different strength
