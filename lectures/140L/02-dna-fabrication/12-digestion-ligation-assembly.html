Setting up a Ligation Reaction

Ligation of Purified, Digested DNAs

THIS IS AN EXAMPLE PROTOCOL. FOLLOW THE PROTOCOL ON YOUR INDIVIDUAL WORKSHEET

Set up the following reaction in a PCR tube, in the following order:

6.5 uL ddH20
1   uL T4 DNA Ligase Buffer
1   uL Vector digest
1   uL Insert digest
0.5 uL T4 DNA Ligase
  1. Pound upside down on the bench to mix thoroughly
  2. Give a quick spin to send it back to the bottom of the tube
  3. Run the appropriate PCR program on your individual worksheet

Example PCR program

Run the "T4ligation" program on the thermocycler:

25°C for 30 minutes
 4°C forever

(or later)
