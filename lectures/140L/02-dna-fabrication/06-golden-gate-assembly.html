Design of a Golden Gate junction

  • Construct the sequences of the fragments you are joining
  • Pick the 4 bp junction — add 4 bases, take the last 4 of one fragment, or straddle the boundary
  • Copy and paste the BsaI sites and 5’ tails
  • Find ~20 bp of exact match past the junction — and past any mutation
  • Keep those two strands — and write the reverse one 5’ to 3’
