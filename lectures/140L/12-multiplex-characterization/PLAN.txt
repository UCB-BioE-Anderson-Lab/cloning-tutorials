WHY NO WHITE-GROUND RENDER WAS NEEDED
--------------------------------------
Unlike lectures 10 and 11, this source deck already puts its content on
WHITE.  Only the title and the section dividers use the blue master.  So
the figures are cropped straight from a render of the untouched pptx --
no recolour_pptx.py, no build_white.py, no inking pass, and therefore
none of the halo and speckle problems those passes caused in lecture 11.

The crops are the union of every picture's bounding box on a slide, read
from each p:pic's a:xfrm and converted at 200 dpi (EMU / 4572 on this
9144000 x 6858000 page).  Anchor on a:xfrm, never on .//a:ext -- a:ext
is also the extension-list element and matches first.

SOURCES 15 TO 19: DONE, 2026-11-16
-----------------------------------
This was the biggest gap in the rough cut.  Five slides carried the
Illumina construct as one undifferentiated run of ~660 monospace
characters; in the source every region is COLOURED and five of them are
LABELLED with their own text boxes, so the conversion had kept the raw
bases and thrown away everything they meant.

seq/15-illumina.js now draws, on each of the five, a MAP of the
construct with the regions named, and under it the ACTUAL SEQUENCE
coloured by region.  The map is schematic -- segment widths are chosen
so the labels fit and the middle is compressed hard.  The sequence is
exact: all five were diffed against the runs read out of
ppt/slides/slide1[5-9].xml and are character-for-character identical
(664, 664, 660, 192, 192).  Re-check the totals if you edit one.

THREE FAULTS IN THE SOURCE'S OWN COLOURING ARE FIXED, and because
these are changes to JCA's slides they are written out in full in the
file's header.  In short: the two "MiSeq Primer Binding" labels had
different colours; accent4 meant both the spacer and one of those
primer sites; and the 20 bp library-specific sites -- the thing source
15's note is actually about -- were drawn in bg1, white on white, and
so could not be seen at all.  One thing is added: the open reading
frame at the end of 18 and 19 is coloured, because source 18's whole
argument is that the read runs out before the gene can identify itself
and that is invisible while the gene is buried in a run of N.

Sources 17-19 draw the architecture desaturated and only the barcode
(and on 19 the T7 site) at full strength, which is the source's own
change of emphasis on those slides, kept.
