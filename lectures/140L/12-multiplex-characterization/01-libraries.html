Types of Libraries

Error-Prone Libraries

  • Random point mutations

Alanine Scan Libraries

or more generally Site Saturation Variant Libraries (Twist)

  • Mutate each site of the gene to alanine (or other AAs), one mutant per gene

Saturation Libraries

  • Multiple specific sites, a specific set of mutations at each site, in combinations

Expression Libraries

Genomic DNA libraries

  • Fragments of a genome or environmental sample of genomes

http://www.nature.com/nature/journal/v409/n6822/images/409860bw.2.jpg

Oligo Pools

  • Tiny amounts of many oligos mixed in one tube
  • Multiple suppliers using different technologies, price points, and quality
  • Can be much longer than traditional CPG oligos
