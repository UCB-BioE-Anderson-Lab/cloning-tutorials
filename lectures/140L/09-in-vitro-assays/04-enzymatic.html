Filter binding assays

  • Dispense reaction on TCA-soaked filter paper
  • Wash with 10% TCA multiple times to remove small molecules
  • Exchange out water with ethanol
  • Dry out ethanol with ether
  • Measure radioactivity with a scintillation counter
