glgS troubles

Susan did a markerless glgS knockout of MG1655, a second knockout with CmR of the rfaE gene in another MG1655, and then tried to move the rfaE::Cm marker into the glgS knockout strain by P1 transduction. Her products only have rfaE knocked out. What happened?

In PubMed, protein-search each gene, then navigate from the protein to the CDS to the GenBank sequence

Look up where the two genes actually are:

glgS in MG1655   NC_000913  REGION: 3191739..3191939
rfaE in MG1655   NC_000913  REGION: 3195320..3196753

Drawn to scale, that is the whole answer:

You cannot move one without the other. The fragment that brings rfaE::Cm in brings the donor’s wild-type glgS with it, and recombination puts both in — so selecting chloramphenicol selects for exactly the event that undoes her first knockout.
