fimA knockout result

Predict the product of the genome locus from the previous experiment, after integration and after Flp excision of CmR

The locus is fimA with its neighbours — the file is in the Genome Editing Examples zip

1. Predict the PCR product of dwF and dwR on pKD3.

2. Find where its two forty-base ends match the genome, and recombine over them. Everything between those two matches is replaced.

3. For the Flp product, loop out everything between the two FRT sites and leave one behind.

The three sequences are in the speaker notes, and in the zip.
