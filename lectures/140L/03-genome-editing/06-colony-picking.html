Picking colonies

  • Pick well-rounded, isolated colonies representing distinguishable phenotypes
  • For pP6 it’s the greenest things you find, or other interesting phenotypes
  • Usually 2 to 4 colonies
  • Photograph the plate for records
  • Fill a 15 mL conical with 4 mL of 2YT containing antibiotic (carb)
  • Stab or scoop with a sterile tip or toothpick, then drop the utensil in
  • Incubate with shaking overnight
