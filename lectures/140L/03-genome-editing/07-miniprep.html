Miniprepping

Miniprep kit

Alkaline lysis

  • Fill a 2 mL tube with saturated culture
  • Spin 1 min, toss the supernatant (bleach, then drain)
  • Resuspend in 250 uL P1 — Tris, EDTA, RNase A
  • Lyse with 250 uL P2 — NaOH, SDS
  • Neutralize with 350 uL N3 — guanidinium chloride, acetate
  • Spin 5 min

Column cleanup

  • Transfer the clarified supernatant to a column
  • Spin 15 s, discard — the DNA is now bound to the column
  • Add 500 uL PB
  • Spin 15 s, discard — protein is removed
  • Add 750 uL PE
  • Spin 15 s, discard — salts are removed
  • Spin 90 s to dry the column
  • Add 50 uL water
  • Spin 45 s into a fresh tube, discard the column
