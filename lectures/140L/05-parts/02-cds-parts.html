Signallingkinases, phosphatases, two-component systems

Structuralcytoskeleton, polymers, the shape of the cell
Chaperoneswhat folds, and what gets a second attempt
Degrons and proteaseswhat persists, and for how long
Condensate driverscompartments with no membrane around them

None of these is a chemical category. A kinase is an enzyme and a signalling protein at once, and knowing it is a kinase does not tell you which pathway it is in.
