# Media and Plates

How the lab's liquid media, antibiotic stocks and agar plates get made, in the order you would make
them. Each step links to its protocol in the [Protocol Builder](../protocols/protocols.md), which
does the arithmetic for your batch size, and has a video of it being done in B144.

Pouring plates uses a Bunsen burner and is **supervisor-only**; see
[Plate Pouring](./plate_pouring.md). Re-melting agar needs the [Microwave](./microwave.md)
training.

## 1. LB agar

Bottles of LB agar, sterilized in the pressure cooker. They set solid and keep until you need
plates.

[LB agar protocol](https://ucb-bioe-anderson-lab.github.io/cloning-tutorials/protocols/protocols/?id=preparation_of_lb_agar&autogen=1)

## 2. 2YT broth

The liquid medium for growing cultures and for the rescue step after a transformation. Made the
same way as LB agar, without the agar.

[2YT broth protocol](https://ucb-bioe-anderson-lab.github.io/cloning-tutorials/protocols/protocols/?id=preparation_of_2yt_broth&autogen=1)

## 3. Antibiotic 1000× stocks

One video per antibiotic. Weigh roughly 50 mg, record the mass you actually got, and let the
protocol work out the solvent volume from it. Choose the antibiotic in the protocol and it shows
the matching video.

[Antibiotic stock protocol](https://ucb-bioe-anderson-lab.github.io/cloning-tutorials/protocols/protocols/?id=preparation_of_antibiotic_1000x_stock&autogen=1&antibiotic=carbenicillin)

## 4. Re-melting agar

A set bottle of LB agar goes back into the microwave in short bursts. It boils over fast.

[Re-melting protocol](https://ucb-bioe-anderson-lab.github.io/cloning-tutorials/protocols/protocols/?id=remelting_lb_agar&autogen=1)

## 5. Pouring plates (supervisor-only)

Cool the agar, add the antibiotic, pour, and mark the plates by antibiotic.

[Plate pouring protocol](https://ucb-bioe-anderson-lab.github.io/cloning-tutorials/protocols/protocols/?id=pouring_petri_dishes&autogen=1&plates_needed=20)

## 6. Sealing plates with parafilm

Sealed plates keep in the fridge without drying out. The same wrap goes on any plate you keep
after picking colonies.

[Parafilm protocol](https://ucb-bioe-anderson-lab.github.io/cloning-tutorials/protocols/protocols/?id=parafilm_sealing_plates&autogen=1)

<div class="lab-video" data-video="parafilm"></div>

## New plates need controls

Plates poured with anything other than carbenicillin are unproven until a control grows on them.
See [Control Strains and Plasmids](../wetlab/controls.md).
