What you should leave with

What is actually scarce

Not people who can write Python. Not people who know the facts.

People who can look at a piece of biology and say precisely what it is — precisely enough that a machine can act on it, and well enough to know when the machine is wrong.

Four things

Formalize a domain — the nouns, a schema, the operations, and what it discards
Encode and calibrate a constraint — a biological fact as a predicate with a defensible threshold
Judge an artifact biologically — recognize output that runs cleanly and is false
Compose all three into something someone else can use

One sentence

Represent biological systems precisely enough that machines can act on them — and recognize when the machine is wrong.
