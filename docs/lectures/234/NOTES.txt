BioE 134/234 DECKS — what differs from 140L
===========================================

The format, the tooling and every trap are the same; read ../AUTHORING.txt
first.  deck.css, deck.js, the two text channels, lecture.js as the single
place section order lives, tools/check_section.js and tools/make_pdf.sh all
work here unchanged.  A section file in 234/NN-name/ reaches the shared
assets at the same depth 140L does:

    <link rel="stylesheet" href="../../deck.css">
    <script src="../../deck.js" defer></script>

Only these three things are particular to this course.

NOTHING ON THIS PAGE IS DATED.  index.html carries titles and topics and no
calendar: no due dates, no office hours, no week numbers, no year.  Which
lecture falls in which week is a property of a semester, not of the course,
and it lives in the course plan and on bCourses.  A deck that names a date
has to be edited every year, and will not be.

TWENTY-THREE LECTURES, NOT TWENTY-FIVE.  The teaching plan has twenty-five
meeting slots, but two of them are activities with no deck — the project
spec workshop and the final exam review — and the teaming half of the
project-ideas lecture is also semester mechanics.  They are absent here on
purpose.  The site lists content; the plan lists the term.

FOLDER NUMBERS ARE BUILD ORDER.  Same as 140L, where lecture 2 lives in
01-dna-enzymes.  The syllabus position is the number in <span class="n"> in
index.html, and the two are not expected to match.  Order is an argument
this course is still having; build order is just a fact about what exists.

ADDING A LECTURE
----------------
1. Make 234/NN-name/ with a lecture.js, and a seq/ folder if it needs one.
2. Write lecture.js — it is the only place section order lives.
3. Turn that lecture's <article class="lec todo"> in index.html into a link
   to its first section, and add the <details> list of sections.
