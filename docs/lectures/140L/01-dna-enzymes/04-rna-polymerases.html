RNA Polymerases

  • Enzymes that initiate a new RNA chain on a DNA template
  • There are RNA-directed RNA polymerases as well (in some viruses)

T7 RNA Polymerase

  • Used for both in vivo and in vitro transcription
  • Very high protein expression in vivo
  • Production of tRNAs, mRNAs, or ribozymes/aptamers in vitro
