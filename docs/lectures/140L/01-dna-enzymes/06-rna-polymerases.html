RNA Polymerases

  • Enzymes that initiate a new RNA chain on a DNA template
  • There are RNA-directed RNA polymerases as well (in some viruses)

No Primer

  • Every enzyme so far could only extend an end that already existed

The T7 Promoter and the +1 Site

  • Seventeen bases, quoted on the non-template strand

Elongation: the Bubble Travels

  • NTPs, not dNTPs — and U wherever the template reads A
  • The product leaves single-stranded; the duplex closes behind it

Run-off Transcription

  • Linearise downstream of the insert, and digest to completion
  • Cut to a blunt or 5’ overhang end — a 3’ overhang invites antisense product

Orthogonality: a Private Address

  • Neither polymerase can read the other’s promoter

T7 promoter

5’-TAATACGACTCACTATAG…-3’

17 bases. Read by T7 RNA polymerase, a single polypeptide that needs nothing else.

E. coli σ70 promoter

5’-TTGACA…17 bp…TATAAT-3’

Two elements, spaced. Read by the host’s multi-subunit enzyme with a sigma factor.

A T7 promoter sits inert in E. coli until you supply T7 RNA polymerase — in a tube, or from the host’s own genome.
