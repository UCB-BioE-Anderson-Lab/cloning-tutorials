Exonucleases

No recognition sequence. What an exonuclease reads is a kind of end.

Four questions define an exonuclease

  • 1 · Which direction does it travel?  5’→3’ or 3’→5’
  • 2 · Single strand or double strand?  ss, ds, or both
  • 3 · Does that end need a 5’ phosphate?  most do not care; Lambda Exo does
  • 4 · What kind of end will it start on?
