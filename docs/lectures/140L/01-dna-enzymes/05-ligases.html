Ligases

  • Join 5’ phosphates to 3’ hydroxyls
  • Requires energy — not a thermodynamically favorable reaction

T4 DNA Ligase

  • No homolog in E. coli
  • Requires 5’ phosphate and 3’ hydroxyl
  • No mismatches or gaps can be present at the junction
  • Operates on any length of homology (though 1bp is worst)
  • Principally used to join DNA ends generated by restriction enzymes

E. coli DNA Ligase

  • Ligase involved in repair/replication in all cells
  • Forms bonds on nicks only
  • Requires a 5’ phosphate
  • Can’t be used to join non-annealed DNA
Which bond is formed… by T4 Ligase

Taq DNA Ligase

  • From Thermus aquaticus
  • Biochemically the same as E. coli DNA ligase, but thermostable
  • Used in the Ligase Chain Reaction (LCR)
    • Similar temperature program to PCR
    • Used to detect polymorphisms
    • Used in some protocols of gene synthesis
  • Used in the Gibson isothermal assembly reaction
