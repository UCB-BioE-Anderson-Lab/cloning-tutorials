The enzyme classes

Five things you can do to a DNA molecule

  • breakEndonucleases · Exonucleases
  • copyDNA Polymerases · RNA Polymerases
  • joinLigases
  • modifyMethyltransferases · Kinases / Phosphatases
  • rearrangeRecombinases

Underneath all five verbs, two reactions

Then it gets complicated

  • One phosphorus, two mechanisms. That is the whole of the chemistry, and it does not change again today — but it is not the whole of the lecture
  • What separates one enzyme from another is where the reaction is allowed to happen — which kind of end, which sequence, which strand, whether the DNA is circular, whether a base carries a methyl
  • That is why a catalogue built on two reactions runs to hundreds of entries, and why picking the wrong enzyme fails silently rather than loudly
  • Everything from here is that question, one enzyme class at a time
