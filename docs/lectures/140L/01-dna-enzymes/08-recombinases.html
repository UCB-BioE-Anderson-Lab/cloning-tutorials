Homologous Recombination

  • No recognition site at all — the homology arms decide where the DNA lands
  • Yeast and B. subtilis do this readily. E. coli does not, which is why you cannot simply transform a linear cassette and expect it to integrate
  • Supply the λ Red genes and it can — that is recombineering, and it is how genes get knocked out in E. coli
  • RecA is the host’s own strand-exchange protein; λ Red substitutes for what E. coli lacks
