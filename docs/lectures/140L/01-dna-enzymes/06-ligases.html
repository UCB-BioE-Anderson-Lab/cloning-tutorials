Ligases

  • Join a 5’ phosphate to a 3’ hydroxyl
  • Requires energy — the reaction is not favorable on its own
  • Not glue. It is a list of requirements, and every one of them is a way to fail

What a Ligase Requires

T4 DNA Ligase

  • No homolog in E. coli — the phage brought its own
  • Requires ATP — one per bond. Tired buffer is a leading cause of dead ligations
  • The workhorse for joining ends made by restriction enzymes

The other two ligases

  • E. coli and Taq DNA ligase — NAD+, not ATP
  • Both seal nicks only. Not blunt ends, and not 4-base sticky ends
  • The native job: repair, and sealing Okazaki fragments
  • It is the ligase in Gibson and in ligase chain assembly — use the thermostable one
  • Run hot, Taq ligase also refuses a mismatched junction, sharpest at the 3’-OH
