DNA Polymerases

Enzymes that add dNTPs to recessed 3’ DNA ends (5’ extensions)

The Core Reaction: 5’→3’ Extension

  • One event, drawn three ways — they all mean this

DNA Polymerase I

  • Involved in replication and DNA repair
  • Has multiple domains, each doing different things
  • The “Klenow fragment” includes only the synthesis domain
  • Most thermostable polymerases are homologs of DNA Polymerase I or several subunits thereof

Distinguishing Features

5’→3’ Exonuclease Activity

  • This behavior is pretty rare
  • Taq and E. coli pol 1 have it

Strand Displacement

  • The more common scenario

Rolling Circle Replication

  • Phi29 is the pro at this

Application: Forensic Analysis with RCA

Kunkel Mutagenesis

  • Use T4 DNA Polymerase
  • (no 5’→3’ exo or displacement)

3’→5’ Exonuclease Activity

  • “Proofreading activity”
  • Major component determining fidelity
  • Absent in Taq-like enzymes
  • Present in Pfu-like enzymes
  • Without it, extension products typically contain a single 3’ “A” overhang
