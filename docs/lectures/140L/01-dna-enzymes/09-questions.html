Five things you can do to a DNA molecule

  • breakEndonucleases · Exonucleases
    recognise a site, or recognise an end
  • copyDNA · RNA Polymerases
    extend a 3’ end — or, with a promoter, start from nothing
  • joinLigases
    seal a nick, never a gap, and only if the ends are held together
  • modifyMethyltransferases · Kinases / Phosphatases
    write an annotation, or grant a licence to be ligated
  • rearrangeRecombinases
    swap two sites — their orientation picks the outcome
