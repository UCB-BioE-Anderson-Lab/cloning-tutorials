A closer look at pTargetF

Designing a gRNA

  • Download the genome sequence — here cscR, GenBank CP002185.1
  • cscR regulates sucrose uptake in E. coli W and Mach1, our strain, so we may want it gone

Find a GG

  • Cas9 needs a PAM, so the target has to be followed by GG — these are your options in cscR
  • On the coding strand. You can also look for CC, which are targets on the non-coding strand

And ideally start with an A

  • The guide is transcribed from a sigma70 promoter, which prefers to start on an A
  • It also helps to pick a spacer with no internal GG
  • So search for AnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnGG and take your pick

Put the protospacer in these oligos

Forward primer
         SpeI       protospacer         guide scaffold
5’-ccataACTAGTacgctgcctgagtctctgtagttttagagctagaaatagcaag-3’

Reverse primer
         SpeI       J23119
5’-ctcagACTAGTattatacctaggactgagctag-3’

Only the red changes. Everything else is the same in every guide you will ever order.

pTargetF’s original protospacer, for you to replace

Search AnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnGG in fimA. One hit is AAACGTTGGTGTGCAGATCCTGG — the first twenty bases are the spacer, and the TGG after them is the PAM, which stays in the genome and never goes in the oligo.
