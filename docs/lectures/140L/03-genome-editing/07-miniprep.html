Alkaline lysis

  • Fill a 2 mL tube with saturated culture
  • Spin 1 min, toss the supernatant (bleach, then drain)
  • Resuspend in 250 uL P1 — Tris, EDTA, RNase A
  • Lyse with 250 uL P2 — NaOH, SDS
  • Neutralize with 350 uL N3 — guanidinium chloride, acetate
  • Spin 5 min
