φ80 is the one in your example files

  • Orthogonal. Each integrase only recognises its own att pair, so several can be used in one strain to build at several loci
  • Most carry up to about 10 kb. φC31 takes more, which is why it is the one used in mammalian cells
  • The integrase is supplied in trans, from a helper plasmid, and then cured
  • CRIM packages all of this: Conditional-Replication, Integration, Excision and Retrieval plasmid-host systems

No pUC for CRIM

Why can’t you use a normal pUC plasmid for doing CRIM integration?

Because it would replicate — so every transformant keeps the marker whether or not it integrated, and there is nothing to select.
