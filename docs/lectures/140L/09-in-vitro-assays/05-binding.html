Footprinting

  • Make a PCR product of the DNA with biotin on the 5’ end
  • Incubate it with the binding protein
  • Add dilute DNAse I. It cuts where the DNA is not protected.
  • Quench the reaction and isolate the DNA fragments with streptavidin
  • Run a gel
  • Photocrosslinking is a related method for mapping binding sites in protein:protein interactions
