CONVENTIONS CARRIED OVER FROM THE WHOLE-CELL DECK
-------------------------------------------------
Read that deck's PLAN.txt before touching this one; it was settled the
hard way and the same rules apply here.
  - A NOTE IS WHAT HE SAYS.  First person, students addressed as "you",
    no stage directions.  Source notes are verbatim; a note may be
    SPLIT across clicks but never reworded, and the split is proved by
    concatenating it back.
  - SECTION DIVIDERS ARE A TITLE ON THE AMBER GROUND, with no picture.
    A source image on a coloured ground is a white box and reads as a
    mistake.
  - FIGURES MUST PICTURE REAL THINGS.  No shape standing for an idea.
  - MEASURE PAINTED WIDTH, not the <img> element: nearly every figure
    is object-fit:contain in a flex box, so the element is as wide as
    its column while the picture is letterboxed to the box's height.
  - Readings invented for an illustration are labelled illustrative.

TWO SETS OF SLIDES ARE DRAWN, NOT CROPPED
-----------------------------------------
SOURCE 5-9, because they are motion paths.
The one place the literal pass could not be literal.  Source 7, 8 and 9
are MOTION PATH animations: the cell's contents travel out to an Extract
zone or down to a Debris Pellet, and WHICH contents go WHERE is the whole
argument of each slide.  LibreOffice renders a motion-path shape where it
STARTS, so a render of those slides is every object sitting in its home
position with the "Extract" label lying across the cell.  There is no
still to crop, and variants.py cannot help: it deletes shapes that arrive
later, which captures entrances and nothing else.

So the bacterium is drawn once, in seq/cell.js, and its parts move.  One
drawing serves five slides (cell5 .. cell9).  The contents, the labels,
the destinations and the order are the source's; the notes are verbatim.

SOURCE 43, because its shapes share names.  It builds the ELISA sandwich
in six clicks, a layer at a time — but several of its shapes carry the
same cNvPr name, so deleting "the shapes that arrive later" deletes the
wrong ones: three of the seven states render empty and one jumps two
layers.  There is no intermediate state to crop.  seq/elisa.js redraws
it, layer for layer, and the six labels are HTML beside it rather than
type baked into a bitmap.

SOURCE 35-38, because of the colour.  These are JCA's own drawings and
they convert cleanly in SHAPE — but they were drawn for the grey master,
in a pale blue (#8AB0DD) and a pale yellow that read on dark grey and
nearly vanish on the white ground this deck uses.  Cropping carries that
colour over.  They also build across ten states between them, including
an EXIT on 38 (the unreacted hot tyrosine washing out of the paper),
which no render can give you.  seq/aminoacyl.js redraws them in the house
palette, shape for shape: the synthetase is still a pac-man, the tRNAs
are still cloverleaves, the hot tyrosine is still one dot, the TCA paper
is still a tan disc.

A slide that also carries a list arriving on its last click (7, 9) or a
closing line (8) keeps that in HTML beside the stage: cell.js writes
data-step on the slide and the section's CSS reads it, because a slide
with data-seq has its data-build attributes ignored by deck.js.

TWO DELIBERATE DEPARTURES FROM VERBATIM, both proved by concat.py:
  source 4  the image credit ("Image from handling-solutions.eppendorf

STRUCTURES ARE REDRAWN WITH RDKIT
---------------------------------
tools/draw_reaction.py takes two SMILES and writes the reaction as SVG.
It lays the PRODUCT out first and then fits the substrate onto the
product's coordinates through their maximum common substructure, so both
are drawn in the same orientation and the only thing that moves between
them is the group the enzyme put there.  That is the whole point of such
a picture, and two independent depictions lose it.

Every SMILES came from PubChem by name, and each structure carries its
published molecular formula, which the tool asserts before it draws.
The carotenoid chart's ten structures and their CIDs are in
spec/carotenoids.json.

  14  guanidinium chloride                      molecule
  31  p-nitrophenyl phosphate -> p-nitrophenol  reaction, alkaline phosphatase
  32  theobromine -> caffeine                   reaction, caffeine synthase
  33  ATP, alpha phosphorus marked 32P          molecule, with alpha/beta/gamma
  49  the ten carotenoids                       grid

A FORMULA CHECK IS NOT ENOUGH, and this deck proved it.  A SMILES that
reuses a ring-closure digit the scaffold has already opened fuses two
rings into one macrocycle while leaving every atom and bond count
identical, so the formula still matches and only the picture is wrong.
That is what happened to the Whole-Cell deck's Sgal.  chemdraw.py takes
--contains for that: assert the fragments that must really be present.

RDKit's own font has no Greek letters, so its atomNote lands in the SVG
as an empty path and nothing shows.  ATP's alpha, beta and gamma are
drawn by the tool from the phosphorus atoms' own drawing coordinates, in
the deck's typeface.

Source 33's ATP bitmap and source 31's construct cartoon were cropped
from the same slide as the structures they sat beside, so both crops
carried a tail of the structure above them.  Re-cropped once the
structures became vector.

SIX STATEMENTS WERE CORRECTED, with the reason:
  17  "proteins that do not contain histidine residues do not bind" --
      nearly every protein has a histidine; it is the six in a row that
      hold on.  Also, the metal ion is held by a chelator that is
      covalently attached, not covalently attached itself.
  21  the note described magnetic deflection next to a list of
      instruments that mostly do not use it (a quadrupole filters on an
      oscillating electric field, TOF times the flight, an Orbitrap uses
      an electrostatic orbit).  Now scoped to the instrument drawn, with
      the others named.
  24  splitting was attributed to coupling with H's on the SAME carbon;
      it is coupling to the neighboring carbon's hydrogens.
  28  "ketones aren't abundant in primary metabolism" -- pyruvate,
      oxaloacetate, alpha-ketoglutarate and fructose all are.  The
      sentence now claims only what is true of isolated alkenes.
  32  "This molecule isn't detectable by photometry" -- caffeine absorbs
      strongly near 273 nm, and the chromatogram on this very slide has
      an absorbance axis with a labeled caffeine peak.  The real point is
      that substrate and product share a chromophore.
  45  hydroxylamine is a mutagen rather than a standard footprinting
      probe; replaced with dimethyl sulfate and hydroxyl radical.
  46  the textbook was named "principles of analytical chemistry".  JCA
      confirmed he means Skoog's "Principles of Instrumental Analysis",
      which is also the book the ion-chromatography figure credited on
      source 12 comes from.
