WHY NO WHITE-GROUND RENDER WAS NEEDED
--------------------------------------
Unlike lectures 10 and 11, this source deck already puts its content on
WHITE.  Only the title and the section dividers use the blue master.  So
the figures are cropped straight from a render of the untouched pptx --
no recolour_pptx.py, no build_white.py, no inking pass, and therefore
none of the halo and speckle problems those passes caused in lecture 11.

The crops are the union of every picture's bounding box on a slide, read
from each p:pic's a:xfrm and converted at 200 dpi (EMU / 4572 on this
9144000 x 6858000 page).  Anchor on a:xfrm, never on .//a:ext -- a:ext
is also the extension-list element and matches first.

THE FIRST THING TO FIX: SOURCES 15 TO 19
-----------------------------------------
Five slides carry the Illumina construct as a single ~700-character run
of bases.  In the source these are COLOURED and LABELLED -- P5 adapter,
MiSeq primer binding, variable-length spacer, P7 adapter, and on 17-19 a
barcode picked out in green -- and the labels are separate text boxes
pointing at regions of the sequence.  The rough cut renders the run as
one monospace block and loses all of it, which makes those five slides
close to meaningless.  They want the lecture-11 treatment: the sequence
set as HTML with each region in its own colour, and the region names as
labels.  That is the single biggest gap in this pass.
