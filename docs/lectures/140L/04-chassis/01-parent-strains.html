So what should a chassis contain?

A car stripped to the absolute minimum moves, under one set of conditions. Nobody builds on one.

engine and drivetrain
the prototrophic core
steering, brakes, suspension
regulation and defence
standard mounting points
the parts you will add
the body
whatever you are actually building

A chassis is not the minimum that replicates. It is that plus the layers that keep it running once conditions shift and your genes are in it.
