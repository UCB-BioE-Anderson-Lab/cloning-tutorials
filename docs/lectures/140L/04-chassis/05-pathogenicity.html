Toxins — and PAMPs, which are not the same thing

PAMPs — pathogen-associated molecular patterns

  • Endotoxin (LPS), lipoprotein, non-CpG DNA, fMet proteins
  • Structural features of ordinary prokaryotic biology
  • Elicit strong immune responses — and cause sepsis
  • But not virulence factors as such

Toxins — which are

  • Shiga toxin, botulinum toxin, diphtheria toxin, hemolysins
  • Actively damage host cells: lysing them, or entering and stopping translation
Drinking bacteria

Jimmy drank a flask of MG1655. Is he going to die?

No. Lab strains are harmless by mouth — they will not survive the trip, and they carry no toxin that can be delivered that way.

Injecting bacteria

Jill injected herself with MG1655. Is she going to die?

She might. Around 1×109 CFU — one millilitre of saturated culture — injected into a human is where sepsis begins. Not a toxin. The LPS.
