MG1655 / W3110 lineage — and B, and W

  • Most lab strains descend from one isolate, K-12
  • MG1655 and W3110 are nearly identical derivatives of it
  • BL21 is not a K-12 strain at all — it is E. coli B
  • W is a third lineage, used industrially and in some competent cells
