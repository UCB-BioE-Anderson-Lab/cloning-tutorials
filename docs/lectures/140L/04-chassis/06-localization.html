Periplasmic expression

Some of these the protein forces on you. The rest you choose, because everything after the incubator gets easier.

the protein decides

  1. It is toxic in the cytoplasm
    and perfectly well behaved on the other side of the membrane
  2. It cannot fold in the cytoplasm
    the periplasm is oxidising, so disulfides form — antibody fragments are the standard case
  3. It is on its way somewhere else
    anything bound for the outer membrane or the outside goes through here first

you decide

  1. You want the real N-terminus
    the peptidase cuts at a defined site, so no initiator methionine is left on the front
  2. You want a cleaner lysate
    the periplasm is a few per cent of the cell's protein, and osmotic shock releases it on its own
  3. You are displaying it
    phage and surface display both export through here — there is no cytoplasmic version

Getting it there is a prepro signal on the front, and you take one that already works — pelB, ompA, ompT. A periplasmic leader peptidase, LepB for most proteins and LspA for lipoproteins, cuts it off on arrival.

Outer membrane localization

Beta barrel-type proteins

  • OmpA, OmpT, OmpG, LamB
  • Start with sec secretion to the periplasm
  • Then spontaneously insert into the outer membrane

Autotransporters / two-partner secretion

  • Ag43 is the well-studied example
  • Often a cleaved “passenger” — type V secretion
  • A dedicated secretion system for a single protein
  • Also begins with sec secretion

I want to express alkaline phosphatase from Thermus thermophilus (CAC83793.1) in the periplasm of E. coli. Do I need to alter the CDS to get proper localization?

No. It already carries a signal peptide — and look again, because it is a Tat signal, not Sec. E. coli has a Tat system too.
