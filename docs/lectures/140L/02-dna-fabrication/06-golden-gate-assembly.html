Golden Gate from Plasmids

One vector per bin, each has matching, unique 4bp overhangs:

  • Build basic parts in plasmids and sequence-confirm
  • Assembly them by pooling the plasmid DNA, then digest (BsaI) and ligate (T4 Ligase) simultaneously

Design of a Golden Gate junction

  • Construct the sequences of the fragments you are joining
  • Add 4 bp as the annealing region, or pick the last 4 bp of one of the two fragments, or have it flank the junction
  • Copy and paste the BsaI sites and 5’ tails
  • Find a ~20 bp annealing region on the fragment ends
  • Your oligos are in black
