Is Lilly inside claim 1?

Claim 1 of the ’525 patent “A recombinant plasmid replicable in procaryotic host containing within its nucleotide sequence a subsequence having the structure of the reverse transcript of an mRNA of a vertebrate, which mRNA encodes insulin.”

Eli Lilly and Company makes human insulin in bacteria. Walk the claim. One element at a time.

Regents of the University of California v. Eli Lilly & Co., 119 F.3d 1559 (Federal Circuit, 1997). The University of California — inventors at the San Francisco campus, patent held by the Regents.

So does UC win?

The specification disclosed the rat insulin cDNA sequence. For human, it gave a general method and the amino acid sequences of the A and B chains. No human nucleotide sequence appears in the patent.

NO The claims were invalid for want of written description — and so the court never reached whether Lilly infringed them.
