The back of the envelope

  1. 1
    How many units a year? Not the market you can imagine — the one you can reach, and that is allowed to buy it.
  2. 2
    What will the buyer actually bear? And who is the buyer? Often not the person who benefits.
  3. 3
    What does one unit cost to make? From the process diagram, not from a guess. Count the release testing.
  4. 4
    Does that cost fall as the number rises? This is the one that decides. If the answer is no, you have a service wearing a product's valuation.

Then: how much capital, and how many years, before the first unit is sold?
