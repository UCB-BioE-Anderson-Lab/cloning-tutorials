rbs.CDS Parts

  • Secondary structure between an rbs and a CDS is a major determinant of expression
  • Thus it may be more robust to objectify the Transcript (rbs and CDS together) rather than the individual features as parts
  • If the CDS is from bacteria, nature has already selected a working combination
  • Also allows for a reduction in combinatorial complexity; a gene can be composed from only 2 parts: 1 TPcon and 1 rbs.CDS
