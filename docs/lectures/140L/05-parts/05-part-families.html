Twelve is not twelve times the work — it is one pooled reaction and one plate, so about twice. What you buy is taking a guess off the critical path.

TPcon — an architecture built for swapping

rbs.CDS — a family whose members are composites

→§3 said define the transcript as the part. Here is what that buys in a family.

  • The number you look up is the number you get. The site was characterised in front of this gene, which is the only context in which its strength means anything.
  • A bacterial gene arrives pre-optimised. Evolution already selected a 5′ region that folds acceptably in front of that coding sequence. You are taking the answer rather than searching for it.
  • Two parts per gene instead of three. Which is the difference between a library you can build and one you cannot.

Not the seven best BLAST hits — those are one protein seven times. A spread, so that whatever turns out to matter, some member has it.

And say in advance how you will tell an inactive ortholog from an unexpressed one. If you cannot, write that down — naming the limit beats asserting past it.

Five ways to make a family

randomise a slot
pP6, the Anderson library. You do not know what the members will be until you measure them.
collect natural variants
ortholog panels. Evolution generated the diversity; you are sampling it.
scan a position
alanine scanning at the protein level, synonymous scanning at the DNA level. Systematic, one position at a time, so a result points at a residue.
compose
rbs.CDS. Fold two slots into one where the two were never independent.
standardise
TPcon, MoClo. Not diversity at all — the ends that let any of the others be a family.

The first three make diversity. The last two make it usable, and that is the hard half — shared ends, a shared unit, and no holes.

Where that leaves us

  • A part is a decision, not a sequence. A feature is what a stretch of DNA does; a part is a feature somebody committed to reusing.
  • Ask which molecule does the work. DNA, RNA or protein — not where the consequence lands. That is the four sections we just did.
  • Context dependence is not a defect. An RBS competes with its own messenger; a promoter’s ceiling and its operating point are different questions. The parts abstraction leaks, and it leaks in describable places.
  • A family is the repair. Identical but for one slot, characterised in one unit, with no holes — which turns designing into choosing.

Every failure today looked like a success. Red cells responding to nothing; a verified clone that was dead; a number that would not repeat; six good genes called duds. None threw an error. All were caught by asking what the measurement measured.
