Why will these numbers not repeat?

An origin is a handle, and there are three kinds

→A sequence that makes a whole molecule the substrate of some machine, without encoding any of that machine.

of replication
colE1 · p15A · F · R6K — this molecule gets copied, and at this copy number
of transfer
F · RP4 — this molecule gets pushed into another cell during mating
of packaging
λ · M13 — this molecule gets stuffed into a phage particle

All three are handles: the sequence makes the molecule a substrate, and encodes none of the machine. Supply the machine separately — or the handle silently does nothing.

What a promoter is

Complex promoters

→The same two boxes — plus other proteins’ binding sites arranged around them.

The lac promoter is J23101’s architecture with operators built around it — and its boxes are deliberately poor, because a promoter that is already strong has nowhere to go up.

So read any promoter twice: the boxes set the ceiling, and the sites around them decide where you sit under it.

Four kinds, sorted by what decides

nothing decides
constitutive — the Anderson library, J23101 and its siblings. Always on, at a level you pick when you pick the part.
a small molecule
Pbad arabinose · Plac, Ptac IPTG · Psal salicylate · Ptet. You decide, by what you put in the medium.
a polymerase you supply
T7 · SP6 — invisible to the host’s own polymerase. Off unless you have also delivered the enzyme that reads it.
the cell’s own state
fhuA iron · hdeA oxygen · katG stationary phase. The cell decides, and it will not consult you.

A ladder of who is in control — nobody, you, the cell. And T7’s invisibility to E. coli’s own polymerase is the whole reason a pET plasmid can carry a gene that would kill its host.

Sites that an enzyme reacts on

→Same shape as an origin: a short sequence that makes the molecule a substrate, encoding none of the enzyme.

  • Recombination sites — attB and attP for an integrase, FRT for Flp, hix for Hin. Two sites and the enzyme, and what lies between them is excised, inverted or integrated. Genome Editing §1.
  • Terminal repeats — the Tn5 mosaic end, CTGTCTCTTATACACATCT. A transposase binds one at each end, and the pair plus the enzyme is a transposome that inserts more or less anywhere it is put. Genome Editing §4.

Every part in this section is the same object: a place, not a machine. Which means a handle with nothing to grab it is silent — and there is no assay for that.
