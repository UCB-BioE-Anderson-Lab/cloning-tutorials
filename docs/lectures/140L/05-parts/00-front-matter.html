How many parts is this?

Two words that are not synonyms

feature
a defined stretch of DNA encoding a primitive element of biological functiona property of the sequence — it is a promoter whether anyone ever uses it or not
part
a feature somebody committed to reusing — a name, a number, a tube in a boxa decision somebody made — and nothing in the sequence records it

and these are the kinds — in no useful order, yet:

  • coding sequences
  • promoters
  • RNAs
  • ribosome binding sites
  • terminators
  • origins of replication
  • recombination sites
  • terminal repeats

The same thing written four ways

A Format is a promise about the ends

→A standard biological part is a part whose ends follow a published rule, so that any two parts in the collection can be joined by the same reaction.

and you have already built with these:

  • BioBrick — a fixed prefix and suffix · DNA Fabrication §4
  • BglBricks — the same idea, different enzymes
  • Golden Gate — defined overhangs rather than a prefix · §6
  • MoClo and TPcon6 — Golden Gate, plus a rule about position · §8

They do not agree about where a part stops — and that, not the enzymes, is the commonest reason two standard collections will not compose.
