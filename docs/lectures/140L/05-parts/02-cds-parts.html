What makes a protein a reporter

→A reporter is a protein you can quantify in a living cell without knowing anything about the cell.

“Reporter” is not a kind of protein. Any protein is an enzyme, a binding protein, a transporter, a scaffold — a reporter is one you happen to be able to read.

Two ways to be readable: the protein is the signal, or the protein makes the signal from something you add. The second one amplifies. The first one does not.
