# other cropped figure is quantised to 256 colours.  That takes the directory
# from 15 MB to 4 MB with no visible loss -- these are line art and
# screenshots, and a 200 dpi true-colour PNG of either is almost all noise.

AND A SIXTH, ADDED 2026-11-10
  18-eipcr.png   <- ppt/media/image22.png   (white type on transparency)
  Every label in that figure is WHITE -- it was drawn for a dark slide.
  Cropped from the white render and inked it came out as grey speckle;
  JCA called it blotchy twice.  It is now the untouched media file,
  trimmed and doubled with Lanczos, quantised to 192 colours WITH its
  alpha, and served on a charcoal panel.  Do not add it back to the
  crop list and do not put image22 back in build_white.py INK.
      magick ppt/media/image22.png -trim +repage \
             -channel A -level 22%,100% +channel \
             -filter Lanczos -resize 200% -colors 192 img/18-eipcr.png
  The alpha level lifts the near-transparent JPEG noise off the ground
  WITHOUT painting anything: the glyph edges keep their ramp, the faint
  speckle in the empty areas goes to zero.
  The residual limit is resolution: the original is 558x384, so it is
  soft at slide size.  That is the source file, not the pipeline, and
  only a redraw or a clean copy from the paper fixes it.
