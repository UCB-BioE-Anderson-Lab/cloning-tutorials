Multi-gene knockouts in wild-type strains

Identify, describe, and compare the methods available to generate multiple knockouts in a wild-type strain of E. coli, such as Nissle or pathogenic strains. By this, I mean like there are 4 different genes in the genome, and all 4 are knocked out deliberately. For the options, think through how much work is involved and the efficiency of the process to determine the most practical method.

Microscopes of B144

Tlib3

Review Tlib1 and Tlib2 (the experiments used to make the terminator parts from OLS pools) and design Tlib3 with another group of terminators. You will want to examine the terminators that were chosen for Tlib2 and what worked and what didn’t to justify your choices for the new set. This is a wetlab design project, so follow the tutorial guidelines on how to document it.

If you want to do this, contact JCA for the Tlib1 and Tlib2 data.
