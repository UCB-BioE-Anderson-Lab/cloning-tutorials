THE RULE FOR THIS DECK, AND IT IS THE WHOLE BRIEF
--------------------------------------------------
JCA, 27 Oct 2026: "Don't get creative there -- don't modify the flow.
Not content, just formatting."

That is a ruling, and it was earned.  The In vitro deck before this one
was converted with initiative: figures redrawn into new animations,
closing questions distributed to the ends of sections, speaker notes
written for slides that had none.  It was presented from, and it broke —
the first redistributed question turned out to be unanswerable where it
had been put, and the opening animations were, in his words, a mess.
Both had to be rebuilt against the pptx.

So for THIS deck:

  EVERY slide is converted, in the source's order, including the four
  course-admin slides at the head and the "Questions?" card at the end.
  Dropping a slide is a change to the flow.

  NO slide is split, merged, reordered or moved between sections.

  NO figure is reimagined.  A borrowed raster is cropped and placed.
  A figure is redrawn ONLY where it cannot be cropped at all — a motion
  path has no still to crop — and then shape for shape, not reinvented.

  NO new speaker notes.  17 slides here carry no note (1-5, 7, 11, 38,
  40-43, 45-48, 50) and they stay that way.  Notes are the source's,
  verbatim, with spelling and grammar corrected and nothing else.

  NO new narration, no added sentences, no "better" explanations.

  What DOES change is formatting: the house grounds and type, the house
  palette for the deck's own chrome, section dividers set as dividers,
  and figures cropped off the slide and placed in HTML.

Anything that feels like an improvement is out of scope.  Write it down
here instead and let JCA decide.

TWO DECISIONS MADE WHILE CONVERTING SECTION 01
-----------------------------------------------
Source 10 carries TWO note markers, * and **, but only ONE click.  The
** segment ("We then apply some selective pressure...") is what JCA says
as he advances to source 11, which carries no note of its own.  Moving it
to 11 would put a note on a slide that has none and would fail the
concatenation check against the pptx, so it stays on 10, joined to the
first build's note.  No click was invented to carry it.

Sources 10 and 11 are the one figure in this section drawn rather than
cropped.  They are the same nine ovals and nine arrows in both slides and
differ only in which of them are showing, so a render has them all showing
at once and there is nothing to crop.  The geometry, the fills and the
strokes are lifted shape for shape out of the slide XML.

CHECKING THE NOTES
------------------
  python3 ../../tools/verify_notes.py <unpacked-pptx> --keep-urls *.html

--keep-urls matters here.  The In vitro deck moved its image credits out
of the notes and onto the slides, so a bare leading URL there is a credit
and the verifier strips it.  Nothing is moved in THIS deck, and some of
its notes ARE just URLs — source 8's note is the two links for the
optional structural biology tutorial — so without the flag the verifier
reports a difference that is not there.

TWO TOOL BUGS FOUND WHILE SURVEYING THIS DECK
----------------------------------------------
pptx_text.py opened notesSlideN.xml for slideN.  The notesSlides are
numbered in their own sequence, so the moment one slide has no note the
two numberings part company and every slide after it is handed the note
belonging to a different slide — silently.  This deck has exactly that
shape: slide 1 carries no note, so every note the tool printed was the
NEXT slide's.  Caught because slide 5, a bare title card, appeared to
carry the lecture's opening narration; it belongs to slide 6.  Fixed to
resolve through the rels, as verify_notes.py always did.  Anything read
out of this deck before that fix is suspect and was re-read.

verify_notes.py gained --keep-urls, above.  It also now strips a RUN of
asterisks, not just a single one, because source 10 uses ** for its second
marker and the single-asterisk rule left the stray ** in the comparison.
