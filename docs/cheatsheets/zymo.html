Zymo Cleanup

zymo

Protocol

  1. For each cleanup, top-label a 1.5 mL tube with the name indicated on the labsheet. This is the tube you will elute into.
  2. Add 180 µL ADB (brown bottle) to the reaction and mix.
    • Fragments under 250 bp: use 1 part ADB + 3 parts isopropanol instead of ADB alone, or the fragment washes straight through.
  3. Transfer to a Zymo column in a collection tube. Spin 15 s at full speed, discard the flow-through.
  4. Add 200 µL PE. Spin 15 s, discard the flow-through.
  5. Add 200 µL PE again. Spin 15 s, discard the flow-through.
  6. Spin 90 s at full speed to dry the column. PE is 70% ethanol and any carryover inhibits the ligase.
  7. Move the column to your labelled tube. Add 25 µL EB slowly to the centre of the membrane — do not let it run down the walls.
  8. Spin 45 s to elute. Discard the column.
  • Know where your DNA is at every step. Before each spin, say which half you are keeping.
