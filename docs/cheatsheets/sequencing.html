Cycle Sequencing

sequencing

Protocol

  1. For each read, top-label a 1.5 mL tube with the exact name you put on the submission form. Not PCR tubes. Not an abbreviation, and not a name that only makes sense to you — the facility matches tube to form by that string.
  2. Set up each reaction:
    3G00101 sequencing oligo (2.66 µM)
    10miniprep DNA
    13µL total
    • Our sequencing oligos are standardised to 2.66 µM, so 3 µL is exactly one reaction's worth.
    • Medium copy — pBR322, pAC, and pP6: 10 µL miniprep.
    • High copy — pUC: 4 µL miniprep + 6 µL water.
    • Low copy — pSC101, BACs: PCR the region first and sequence the product.
  3. Specify the dGTP protocol on the submission form. Standard chemistry dies inside a hairpin and will lose the read.
  4. Submit. Results come back in 1–2 days.

Notes

  • Pick a primer upstream of what you want to see — the read starts 20–50 bp downstream and gives 400–1000 bp.
  • Sequence everything you picked, including the boring ones. The middle of the range is the informative part.
