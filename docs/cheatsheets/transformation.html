Protocol

  1. Plates in the incubator first — they are the slow thing. Check they carry the antibiotic(s) on your labsheet. Cold plates carry condensation and you cannot write on a wet plate.
  2. Turn on the EchoTherm: cold block 4 °C, warm block 42 °C. Leave both there throughout.
  3. Once warm and dry, label plate bottoms: date, initials, strain, plasmid, selection.
  4. Put a 100 µL cell aliquot and the DNA tube on the cold block. Thaw ~30 s, add 25 µL KCM to the cells, pipette gently. One aliquot does 3 reactions.
  5. Add 40 µL of the cell/KCM mix to the DNA. Mix gently.
    • Assumes DNA is ~20% of the total; too much dilutes the salts. Retransforming a miniprep: 0.5 µL into 10 µL cells.
  6. Run the block sequence on the timeline above.
  7. Rescue only if not Amp/Carb: 200 µL 2YT, 1.5 mL tube, shake 37 °C 45 min – 2 h. Not less, not more.
  8. Plate everything, spreading with beads. Incubate inverted at 37 °C overnight, and cancel the temperature programs.
