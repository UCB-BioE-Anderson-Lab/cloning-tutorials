Protocol

  1. Start the slow things first. Get your plates — check the antibiotic(s) on your labsheet — and warm them in the incubator. Cold plates carry condensation and you cannot write on them.
  2. Turn on the EchoTherm: block A 4 °C, block B 42 °C. Leave both at temperature throughout.
  3. Once warm and dry, label the bottom of each plate: date, initials, strain, plasmid, selection.
  4. Put a 100 µL cell aliquot and the DNA tube on block A. Thaw ~30 s, add 25 µL KCM to the cells, pipette gently. One aliquot does 3 reactions.
  5. Add 40 µL of the cell/KCM mix to the DNA. Mix gently.
    • Assumes DNA is ~20% of the total; too much dilutes the salts. Large reaction: the whole tube of cells. Retransforming a miniprep: 0.5 µL into 10 µL cells.
  6. Run the block sequence on the timeline above: A 4 °C → B 42 °C → A 4 °C.
  7. If your selection is anything other than Amp/Carb, rescue first: add 200 µL 2YT, move to a 1.5 mL tube, and shake at 37 °C for 1 h.
  8. Plate everything. Incubate inverted at 37 °C overnight, and cancel the temperature programs.
